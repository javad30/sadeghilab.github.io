## About Me

I am an **Assistant Professor** at **Brandon University**, leading research at the intersection of **Microbial Ecology, Evolution, and Genomics**[cite: 1]. My research program—the **Microbial Adaptation & Evolution (MAE) Group**—combines classical microbiology approaches with cutting-edge multi-omics technologies to understand how environmental factors and host genetics shape microbial communities in natural and managed ecosystems[cite: 1].

Focused on the **One Health framework**, my lab investigates host–microbe interactions to enhance environmental and agricultural resilience under climate change[cite: 1]. Our work leverages:
* **Multi-omics approaches** to decipher the functional diversity and metabolic potential of complex microbiomes[cite: 1].
* **Evolutionary genomics** to identify genomic signatures driving the adaptive success of microbial taxa[cite: 1].
* **Plant-microbiome experiments** to explore co-evolutionary dynamics and support sustainable ecosystem health[cite: 1].

I am deeply committed to mentorship, pedagogical innovation, and advancing equity, diversity, and inclusion (EDI) in STEM[cite: 1]. I also serve on the Early Career Editorial Board of *Microbial Biotechnology*[cite: 1].
