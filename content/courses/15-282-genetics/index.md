## Course Overview

**Course Code:** BIOL 15.282  
**Institution:** Department of Biology, Brandon University  
**Instructor:** Dr. Javad Sadeghi  

### Description
This course covers core concepts in modern genetics, spanning Mendelian inheritance, chromosomal mechanics, DNA replication and repair, gene expression, recombinant DNA technologies, and basic population genetics. 

### Key Topics
- Mendelian analysis and extensions of Mendelian principles
- Molecular structure of genes and chromosomes
- Mutation, DNA repair, and recombination
- Quantitative and population genetics
