## Course Overview

**Course Code:** EESC30H3  
**Institution:** University of Toronto  
**Instructor:** Dr. Javad Sadeghi  

### Description
Explores the diversity and activities of microorganisms in natural and managed environments, focusing on nutrient cycling, bioremediation, and plant-microbe symbiosis under changing climatic conditions. Includes hands-on laboratory modules with molecular analysis and data visualization in R.
